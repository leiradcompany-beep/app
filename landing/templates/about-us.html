Our Story

LEIRAD was founded with a simple mission: to provide high-quality, reliable, and eco-friendly home cleaning services. We understand that your home is your sanctuary, and keeping it clean shouldn't be a source of stress.

Our Values

  • Excellence: We strive for perfection in every clean.
  • Sustainability: We use eco-friendly products that are safe for your family and the planet.
  • Trust: All our professionals are thoroughly vetted and trained.
  • Customer Satisfaction: Your happiness is our top priority.

Our Team

Our team consists of dedicated cleaning specialists who are passionate about what they do. We invest in ongoing training to ensure we're always using the best techniques and products available.
